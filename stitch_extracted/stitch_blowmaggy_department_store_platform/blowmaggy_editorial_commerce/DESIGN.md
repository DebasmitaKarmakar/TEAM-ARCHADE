## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-energy, editorial luxury retail department store experience under the ethos: *"Every department. One cart. One checkout."*

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
The visual aesthetic merges the prestige of an upscale physical department store with the kinetic speed and precision of contemporary high-growth digital commerce. It evokes confidence, curation, and effortless scale. Interactions feel sharp, tactile, and frictionless.

### Design Movement: High-Contrast Editorial Modernism
The design system pairs high-contrast editorial typography with structural dark charcoal surfaces and razor-sharp accent boundaries. It avoids generic software neutrals, adopting instead an obsidian-and-alabaster palette punctuated by vibrant, departmental micro-accents. The interface utilizes structured cards, deliberate negative space, razor-thin hairline borders, and polished typographic hierarchies to deliver an elevated, unified multi-category retail environment.

## Colors

The palette anchors on deep obsidian charcoals and high-clarity platinum highlights, complemented by category-specific chroma accents to define cross-store departments.

### Primary Hierarchy
- **Base Neutral (`#0C0D10`):** Obsidian slate acting as the foundation background across canvas surfaces.
- **Surface Elevation (`#15171D`):** Elevated container layer for product cards and structural panels.
- **Primary Content (`#F5F5F7`):** Brilliant off-white for editorial headlines, price indicators, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Muted Content (`#8E929E`):** Mid-tone neutral for metadata, SKUs, brand attribution, and secondary navigation.
- **Hairline Border (`#232631`):** Structural containment line for cards, headers, and division grids.

### Department Chroma Accents
Used strictly for badges, category markers, tags, hover indicators, and unified cart grouping:
- **Fashion:** Scarlet Crimson (`#E02B43`) — primary action and flagship styling.
- **Beauty:** Radiant Peony (`#FF3E83`) — luxury cosmetics and skincare.
- **Electronics:** Cyber Cyan (`#00F0FF`) — precision tech, audio, and personal hardware.
- **Home & Living:** Ochre Amber (`#F59E0B`) — architectural decor and furnishings.
- **Grocery:** Botanical Emerald (`#10B981`) — fresh goods, pantry, and daily provisions.
- **Kids & Toys:** Electric Violet (`#8B5CF6`) — play, dynamic entertainment, and junior apparel.

### Application Guidance
- Category accents must never overpower the core obsidian-and-platinum architecture. Use them with high precision: indicator dots, active tabs, pill border glows, and department identification tags in the unified cart.

## Typography

The typography pairs Italian-inspired editorial serifs with crisp contemporary grotesques and monospaced geometric numerals.

### Font Roles
- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers high-fashion editorial authority across marketing banners, collection headers, and product titles.
- **Body & Description (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Provides frictionless reading clarity for product specifications, store policies, and checkout flows.
- **Labels, Prices & Navigation (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers technical rigor for pricing, department labels, filtering matrices, and checkout summary lines.

### Stylistic Guidelines
- Display headlines use optical sizing with tight tracking to accentuate editorial impact.
- Department tags and status badges always render in uppercase with expanded tracking via `label-badge`.
- Numerical financial data and SKUs consistently employ `Space Grotesk` to align cart tables and summaries cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a structural 12-column grid system built around high-density editorial product presentation.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns, `3rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Max container width capped at 1680px for wide displays.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` margins, `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, `1rem` margins, `0.75rem` gutters. Product feeds reflow into 2-column masonry grids with zero outer card padding to maximize imagery presence.

### Spacing Principles
- Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for tag containment, rating alignment, and metadata lockups.
- Use `space-md` for standard interior card padding and input field separation.
- Use `space-lg` to divide product visual assets from content spec details.
- Use `space-xl` for sectional transitions, department carousels, and modular landing modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through low-luminance tonal layering and crisp hairline containment rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep `#0C0D10` solid backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#15171D` container surface outlined with a crisp `1px solid #232631` hairline border.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Slide-over Cart & Floating Sheets):** `#1A1D24` surface with a subtle directional ambient wash: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Pinned Department Bar):** Obsidian frosted glass with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` at 88% opacity, finished with a `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` edge.

### Lighting & Focus Accents
Interactive cards on hover lift by 2px with an inner specular ring glow tinted by the active department color at 15% opacity, eliminating blur spreads for a precise architectural feel.

## Shapes

The geometric silhouette of the interface is sharp, tailored, and architectural, utilizing soft micro-radii to balance tactile comfort with premium restraint.

### Geometric Scale
- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to product cards, text input fields, square imagery containers, and action buttons.
- **Intermediate Surfaces (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to dialog modals, sliding checkout panels, and collection banners.
- **Micro Badges (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px):** Applied selectively to department badge tags, sale badges, and cart counter pills to create visual distinction from angular card structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Universal Checkout / Main CTA):** Platinum fill (`#F5F5F7`) with deep carbon text (`#0C0D10`), `roundedness: 1`, font `Space Grotesk 600`, letter spacing `0.04em`. On hover, surface inverts to active department accent color with text adapting to contrast.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Cart / Department Filter):** Transparent background with a `1px solid #232631` border, white text. On hover, background shifts to `#1F222B` with border lighting to `#F5F5F7`.
- **Destructive / Remove:** Hairline border with Scarlet Crimson (`#E02B43`) ghost text.

### Department Chips & Filter Badges
- Compact horizontal pills with `Space Grotesk` uppercase typography.
- Inactive state: `#15171D` background with subtle `#232631` border.
- Active state: Charcoal background paired with a `2px` solid indicator in the respective department accent color (e.g., Cyber Cyan for Electronics, Scarlet Crimson for Fashion), accompanied by an illuminated department indicator dot.

### Product Cards
- Contained within a `#15171D` container with a `1px solid #232631` outline.
- **Image Area:** 4:5 aspect ratio, edge-to-edge within the top boundary, framed with an unobtrusive department micro-tag pinned to the top-left.
- **Content Block:** Spaced with `space-md`, featuring `Bodoni Moda` product title, department categorization label in muted slate, and high-contrast `Space Grotesk` price callout.
- **Quick-Add Overlay:** Slides up smoothly from the card base on cursor hover with instant one-click unified cart inclusion.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#111317` with hairline border `#232631`.
- Input font: `Hanken Grotesk 400`, 15px.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#F5F5F7` accompanied by an accent glow keyed to the active flow.
- Placeholders: Muted slate `#636773`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Square (4px radius) for checkboxes; circular for radio options.
- Dark charcoal fill with `#232631` border. On selection, filled with `#F5F5F7` featuring an obsidian checkmark or central indicator.

### Unified Cart & Checkout Module
- A slide-over sheet highlighting the single-cart architecture.
- Grouping: Items are grouped under bold departmental header dividers with their designated chromatic accent bar (e.g., Electronics item group preceded by a Cyber Cyan header line).
- Unified Footer: Single fixed payment summary bar in platinum displaying total item count, combined delivery logistics, and one unified checkout CTA.
